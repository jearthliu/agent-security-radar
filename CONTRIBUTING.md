# Contributing

Paper proposals, corrections, replication notes, and control mappings are welcome.

## Propose an item

Include:

- direct source URL;
- publication or submission date;
- affected lifecycle stage;
- one source-supported fact;
- practical security relevance;
- known limitations or missing evidence.

## Editorial expectations

- Do not copy paper abstracts verbatim.
- Keep facts, interpretation, and unknowns separate.
- Do not inflate benchmark results into production claims.
- Avoid duplicate papers or multiple reports of the same result.
- Never publish credentials, private prompts, personal data, or sensitive exploit details.

Corrections should point to the exact claim and its supporting direct source.
